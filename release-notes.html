  • Official Thunderbird 140 ESR compatibility
  • SmartTemplates is now fully compatible with Thunderbird 140 in preparation for the upcoming annual Thunderbird ESR, expected within the next month (June / July 2025). Users who opt into Thunderbird’s monthly update channel can rest assured: SmartTemplates will continue to deliver compatible, stable releases every month.
  • Modernized internal dialogs: replaced legacy XHTML-based windows with native Thunderbird popups for improved appearance and compatibility. This is part of the ongoing effort of removing legacy (experimental) technology from the Add-on. [issue 378]
Miscellaneous
  • License Management Fix: Clicking 'Extend License' for any license now correctly opens the matching product page, instead of incorrectly opening the New License screen. [issue 377]
